Privacy

Updated 17 September 2026

This covers the Iantha software that connects to your Google account.

What it touches

Files you share with it, and files it creates. Nothing else in your Drive. It reads, edits and organises those documents, spreadsheets and forms, and reads responses to forms it made.

What we do with it

Only what you asked for. We do not sell it, advertise against it, train models on it, or read it ourselves, except to fix a fault you reported or where the law requires it.

Iantha's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we keep

Document contents stay in memory for the length of the operation. What persists is a log of which agent changed which file, and when. Identifiers and timestamps, not content.

Your authorisation credential is stored encrypted and used only to obtain short-lived access tokens.

Stopping it

Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. It takes effect immediately. Email us to have the log deleted too.

The website

iantha.com is static. No cookies, no analytics, no accounts. GitHub and Cloudflare keep short request logs in order to serve it.

Changes

If this changes materially, the date above changes and anyone using the software hears from us first. Iantha is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.
